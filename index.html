42 CFR 435.923 — Authorized Representative
Health centers may submit renewals on behalf of patients who have provided written consent. In Oklahoma the authorized representative form is Authorized representative pathway — exact organizational form/authority must be validated with OHCA before pilot. CoverageGuard IQ captures, tracks, and routes authorized representative status per patient. The platform uses authorized-representative filing mechanism framing throughout — not credential-sharing language.
42 CFR 435.554 / 435.557(f) — Clinician Exemption Attestation
Medically frail exemptions require two elements: Element 1 (qualifying condition) and Element 2 (functional impairment attestation). CoverageGuard IQ surfaces probable exemption candidates from EMR data. A clinician must confirm. The platform uses form CG-FA-01 — confirm MySoonerCare/MySoonerCare accepts a provider attestation or use the state-preferred form.

Manual Gaps
What navigators must do manually today · use these to show the gap — then close with the full platform pitch
CoverageGuard is designed to automate or structure these manual steps where integrations and state permissions allow. Use them during the demo to show the gap.
Part 1 CertCore standalone gaps
Coverage conflict signals invisible — Navigator must manually check pharmacy for rejects, call MCO to verify plan, look up OKSHINE. Each check takes 20–40 minutes per patient.

MySoonerCare vs. MySoonerCare routing — Without the platform, navigators must know agency boundaries by ZIP code. Errors mean wrong portal, wrong form, submission rejected.

Authorized representative form tracking — Auth rep form status tracked in spreadsheets. No automatic flag when a form is missing or expired.
Part 2 Coverage Intelligence gaps without the platform
OKSHINE vs. MCO roster conflicts — Without reconciliation, navigators see contradictions and don't know which source to trust. Average resolution: 45–60 min per complex case per week.

Pharmacy reject classification — A naive system treats every reject as a coverage loss. Two-signal logic (reject + 271) takes a human 30+ minutes to replicate manually.

Revenue at risk invisible — Finance sees denials after they happen. No prospective view of which unresolved conflicts will turn into denied claims.
Part 4 Submission Gateway gaps without the platform
MySoonerCare assister portal navigation — Without the nine-layer checklist, navigators track evidence requirements in spreadsheets. Missing items discovered at submission time.

State response tracking — Navigators manually poll the portal for status updates. RFI deadlines missed because nobody was watching.

Return routing — When the state returns More Information Needed, the case is emailed back. No automatic routing to the correct queue in Part 1.

What to say during the demo when asked about the technology
If asked: "Does Oklahoma have an API?"
"No — MySoonerCare does not expose a public renewal submission API. That is consistent across all four states we are deployed in. CoverageGuard is designed for this reality — it uses the L1 human-assisted portal as the highest available channel, prepares the packet, and guides the submission. When the state makes an API available, the gateway adapts without rebuilding the workflow."
If asked: "Does this automate the portal?"
"No. The navigator logs into the MySoonerCare assister portal using their own credentials. CoverageGuard prepares the evidence package and guides the submission step by step. There is no RPA, no credential sharing, no autonomous portal interaction. The human is always in the loop — that is a governance requirement, not a technical limitation."
If asked: "What about MySoonerCare cases?"
"MySoonerCare-administered cases route to ACCESS MySoonerCare — a separate portal from MySoonerCare. CoverageGuard detects the MySoonerCare routing tag from the patient's ZIP and enrollment record and routes the navigator to the right portal automatically. The nine-layer workbench adapts to the MySoonerCare workflow — different form reference (Authorized representative form), different portal, same structured process."
